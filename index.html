Similarity scores compare the explanation text using deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017 (high reasoning) on a 1–5 scale. They measure semantic agreement, not correctness. Hover over or focus a score to read its rationale.

Scoring criteria
  1. The core explanations are largely unrelated, or clearly contradict each other on key matters such as the core concept or the directions of the positive and negative poles.
  2. There is a small amount of thematic or conceptual overlap, but the accounts differ greatly in their main triggering conditions, function, or positive and negative poles.
  3. The core explanations have substantial overlap, but important differences remain in scope, triggering conditions, positive and negative poles, or mechanism.
  4. The main explanations agree closely. Differences are concentrated in local conditions, secondary patterns, or some mechanistic details.
  5. The substantive explanations are essentially equivalent. The core concept, triggering conditions, and meanings of the positive and negative poles agree; differences mainly concern wording or compatible supplementary details.
